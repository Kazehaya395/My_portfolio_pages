01The path

From keeping work moving to making data useful.

My first professional experience was in office operations, where I handled calls, orders, documentation and day-to-day coordination. It was not a data role, but it taught me how much good work depends on clear information and reliable processes.

I then moved into intelligence analysis. From 2021 to 2025, at iEnergizer Operations in Noida, I analysed online gaming platform data for patterns and anomalies, monitored behaviour for fraud and compliance concerns, assessed risk, prepared intelligence reports and discussed findings with US clients and stakeholders.

Today, I work closer to day-to-day business operations as a Reporting & Operations Analyst with M/S Das & Sons Construction, a government-registered solar vendor. My work includes Excel dashboards, reporting standardisation, recurring documentation, operational data checks and working with field teams to close reporting gaps.

03How I work

Useful work starts before the analysis.

I do not see tools as the starting point. The starting point is understanding what needs to be answered, who will use the result, and what the surrounding workflow actually looks like.

01

Start with the problem

Understand the business or operational question before choosing the tool or technique.

02

Keep it practical

Good analysis has to fit the people, process, data quality and constraints around it.

03

Make complexity understandable

A technically correct result still needs to be clear enough for people to use.

04

Learn by building

I tend to learn new tools fastest when I can apply them to a real problem or working project.
