About me

I didn't start in data.
I got here by staying curious.

My career has moved through office work, IT, intelligence and risk analysis, and reporting. The common thread has been curiosity: understand the problem, understand the system around it, and make the information more useful.

01Who I am

A little more person, a little less job title.

I am naturally curious, fairly detail-oriented, and usually more interested in understanding something properly than in making it look impressive. I like working through a problem until the pieces make sense.

I am also comfortable saying “I don't know yet.” There is a lot to learn in technology and analytics, and pretending otherwise is not very useful. I would rather test something, read the documentation, ask a better question, and improve my understanding.

At work, that usually means being dependable with details, communicating clearly, and trying to leave a process a little easier to follow than I found it.

02The path

From keeping work moving to making information useful.

My first professional experience was in office operations, where I handled calls, orders, documentation and everyday coordination. It was not a data role, but it taught me that small process problems can create much larger headaches.

I then moved into intelligence analysis. From 2021 to 2025, at iEnergizer Operations in Noida, I worked with online gaming platform data to identify patterns and anomalies, support fraud and compliance monitoring, assess risk, prepare intelligence reports, and discuss findings with clients and stakeholders.

Today I work closer to business operations as a Reporting & Operations Analyst with M/S Das & Sons Construction, a government-registered solar vendor. My work includes Excel dashboards, reporting standardisation, recurring documentation, operational data checks and working with field teams to close reporting gaps.
