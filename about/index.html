02 / The story

From keeping systems working to making data useful.

My earlier work in office operations and IT troubleshooting taught me something that still matters: technical work only becomes valuable when people can actually use the result.

From 2021 to 2025, I worked as a Senior Intelligence Analyst at iEnergizer Operations, analysing online gaming platform data, looking for patterns and anomalies, assessing risk, preparing intelligence reports, and discussing findings with US clients.

Today, as a Reporting & Operations Analyst with a government-registered solar vendor, I work closer to day-to-day business operations — building Excel dashboards, standardising reports, automating recurring documentation, checking data against programme guidelines, and helping teams close reporting gaps.

04 / How I work

I care more about useful work than impressive wording.

Start with the problem.I prefer understanding the business or operational question before reaching for a tool.
Keep the workflow practical.Good analysis should fit the people, process and constraints around it.
Make complexity understandable.A technically correct result is not enough if the people using it cannot follow it.
Keep learning by building.I learn new tools best when I can apply them to a real problem or a working project.
