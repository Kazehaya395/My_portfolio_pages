01 / THE QUESTION

Renewable resources are local. Energy systems are connected.

Solar radiation, wind conditions and seasonal temperatures differ from one city to another. A resource that performs well in one place or season may be less reliable elsewhere.

This project explores how city-level weather patterns can help compare renewable potential, estimate heating and cooling demand, and flag conditions that may affect operations.

06 / INTERPRETATION

From a dashboard to better questions.

The value of the project is not just the scores. It is the comparison framework: a way to ask where conditions differ, when patterns change and which assumptions deserve closer examination.

01

Compare locations before generalising

Resource potential and seasonal patterns differ across the cities in the dataset.

02

Make assumptions visible

Scores and downtime flags depend on thresholds and formulas; their definitions should travel with the result.

03

Use findings as planning signals

Storage, maintenance and grid-interconnection ideas are hypotheses to investigate with engineering, cost and grid data—not conclusions from weather data alone.
